| Question style | What to practise |
| --- | --- |
| Describe the steps of an algorithm | Start with its input. Explain the steps in order, why the important decision works, and what the algorithm returns. |
| Explain differences | Compare both methods on the same property. Give a concrete situation where the difference changes the outcome or cost. |
| Do a light derivation with given formulas | Name the symbols. Start with the given formula, show the substitution or rearrangement, and interpret the result. |
