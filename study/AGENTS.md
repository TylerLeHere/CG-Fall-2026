# CSC 305 study decks

When asked to review a lecture or make study materials in this project, create a detailed PowerPoint study deck in this folder, following the lecture 6 and 7 study decks. The user expects a deck as the deliverable.

Read `Final exam format.md` and the source lecture before authoring. Build practice around the instructor's three examples of open-ended questions.

- Describe the steps of an algorithm, including inputs, operation order, reasoning, and output.
- Explain the differences between two concepts or methods on common criteria, with a concrete example.
- Do a light derivation using supplied formulas, defining symbols, showing intermediate steps, and interpreting the result.

Introduce concepts from first principles with worked examples and diagrams. Connect each major topic to a plausible exam question. Label the question type, give the prompt and any required formulas, and put its full worked answer on the following slide. Explain why the steps work and include a check or common error. Practice questions are original exercises based on lecture coverage, not predictions of actual exam questions.

Use readable slides and speaker notes for additional explanation and sources. Save the finished `.pptx` under `study/` with a descriptive lecture-number filename. Preserve the existing study style unless the user requests a redesign.
